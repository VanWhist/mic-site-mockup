FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can I use for free?

Recording, frame-by-frame stepping, slow motion, section loops, air time, flip, zoom, importing from Photos, exporting as recorded, the video library, favorites and reference takes, customizable controls, and Japanese / English switching are all free. Recording and frame-by-frame review stay free.

What's included in FrameReplay Pro?

Six features: compare two takes, lines & angles, replay while recording, continuous mode, export with edits, and importing from an SD card (FlashAir).

Is Pro a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no recurring payment.

Are my videos uploaded to a server?

No. Your videos are stored on your device only. There is no server belonging to us. The only connection the app makes to the internet is to Apple, for the in-app purchase.

Does AI analyze or score athletes?

No. FrameReplay does not score or judge anything automatically. It is a tool for people to watch and decide for themselves.

Which devices and iOS versions are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17.0 or later. Starting a recording from Apple Watch requires watchOS 26.2 or later.

Is MultiCam available now?

Not yet. MultiCam is in development and not included in the App Store version.
