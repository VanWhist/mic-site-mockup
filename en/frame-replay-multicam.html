FrameReplay · MultiCam

Press Record on one device. All cameras record together.

Record the same movement from the front, side and back at once, using several iPhones and iPads.
The videos come together on one device, so you can watch them side by side right away — and one at a time on your own iPhone (a Sub).

Download on the App Store

Connect without a router (between supported devices) or over the same Wi-Fi. No internet needed.

Three roles

Receiving device

Receive in the app

The device you press Record on. All videos come here and can be watched side by side. It also records one angle itself.

Needs Pro and MultiCam

Camera device

Be a camera in the app

Set on a tripod to record another angle. Its videos go to the receiving device.

Free

Sub

Sub (view only) in the app

Videos that arrive on the receiving device also show up on your own iPhone, so a coach, athlete or staff member can watch them one at a time on the spot.

Free

What you need

  • Two or more iPhones or iPads with FrameReplay
  • The latest version of FrameReplay from the App Store on every device (connecting without a router, adding devices by QR code and Subs need version 1.3 or later)
  • Only the receiving device needs a purchase. Camera devices and Subs join with the free version of FrameReplay.
    • The receiving device needs Pro and MultiCam (a one-time add-on to Pro).
    • If you downloaded FrameReplay from the App Store with your current Apple ID before 21 September 2026, Pro and MultiCam are free for you (TestFlight-only use does not count). The app checks this automatically; if it applies, the top of Settings shows "Pro is on for you, as someone who has used the app since 1.0".
  • One way to connect
    • Without a router (Wi-Fi Aware): devices link directly, even where there is no Wi-Fi network. Works on iPhone 12 or later and some iPads with iOS 26 or later. Each pair of devices needs to pair once, the first time.
    • The same Wi-Fi: every device joins one Wi-Fi network. Recommended: a portable Wi-Fi router or Personal Hotspot on an iPhone. Shared Wi-Fi at facilities or hotels often blocks communication between devices. Devices that don't support Wi-Fi Aware connect this way.
  • A tripod or holder for each camera device

How to use it (4 steps)

1

Set up the receiving device

Receiving device
  • In FrameReplay, tap the gear at the top right of the recording screen, then MultiCam under Connections.
  • Set This device to Receive.
  • Enter a Group name (e.g. Team A).
  • Under Name of this device, enter the angle it records (e.g. Front).
  • To connect without a router, turn on Connect without a router (Wi-Fi Aware) (shown only on supported devices). To use the same Wi-Fi, choose the same network in Settings > Wi-Fi on every device (5 GHz if available).
  • When asked about Local Network, tap Allow.
2

Add camera devices and Subs with a QR code

Camera deviceSub
  • Receiving device: on the same MultiCam screen, open Add a device (QR code) and choose Add as (Be a camera / Sub (view only)). A QR code appears.
  • Device you are adding: in FrameReplay, go to Settings > MultiCam > Scan a QR code to join and point it at the receiving device's QR code (the iPhone Camera app works too).
  • On the Join MultiCam screen, check the receiving device, role and group, then tap Join. The role, group and receiving device are set in one go.
  • On a camera device, enter where it stands under Name of this device (e.g. Side, Behind), so you can tell the angles apart.
  • When asked about Local Network, tap Allow.

Adding a device without the QR code

  • On the device you are adding, choose its role under This device, then under Groups nearby tap the button with the receiving device's group (e.g. Join Team A) (when using the same Wi-Fi).
  • When Connection shows "Connected to" and a number (e.g. "Connected to 2"), you're ready.

⚠️ Keep the camera devices on the recording screen.

On any other screen they can't respond when recording starts. (The screen stays on by itself.)

3

Without a router only: pair once, the first time

  • When connecting without a router, the receiving device and each device you add need to pair once, the first time (even if they were paired with a different device before). After joining by QR code, the device you are adding shows the steps.
  • The QR code and pairing are separate steps: the QR code only sets the role, group and receiving device. Connecting without a router also needs pairing. A device added with the QR code of a receiving device that has Connect without a router on needs to pair even when both are on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Receiving device: tap Let others find this device, right below the QR code, and keep that screen open.
  • Device you are adding: tap Pair with a receiving device and choose the receiving device.
  • When a code appears on both screens, check that they match.
  • You're done when the receiving device appears under Pairs this device can use on the device you added. The name shown there is the iPhone or iPad's own name (Settings > General > About > Name), not the name you gave it in FrameReplay. You won't need to pair again next time.
  • If Pairs this device can use still shows None on the receiving device, choose the device from Choose a camera or Sub there.
  • Opening pairing while recording or connected may interrupt the connection. Pair before you start recording.
4

Record from the receiving device

Receiving device
  • Press Record on the receiving device — every camera device starts recording.
  • Stop — every device stops, and the videos are sent to the receiving device automatically. Any Subs get them too.

Connection status (top left of the receiving device's recording screen)

  • Blue dot and a number: how many camera devices are connected (e.g. 1). The number doesn't change while videos are arriving.
  • Yellow dot: looking for camera devices (none connected yet) — "Looking for the other device".
  • Red dot: something needs your attention — "Not connected", "No camera connected" while recording, or a camera device's battery is low (20% or less and not charging), shown in place of the number, e.g. "Side Battery 12%".

Watch your videos

  • On the receiving device, open the video and tap Angles to see 2–4 angles side by side.
  • They are lined up by the time they were recorded. If needed, adjust one frame at a time.
  • Tap the share button to turn the side-by-side view into one video you can save to Photos or send with AirDrop.

Watch on a Sub (view only)

  • Videos that arrive on the receiving device also arrive on each Sub. Any number of Subs can join.
  • Free on a Sub: play each video one at a time, slow motion, frame-by-frame, trim and viewing brightness.
  • Cameras only: this button on a Sub's recording screen makes the camera devices record (the Sub itself doesn't record). The videos go to the receiving device and on to the Sub. Tap it again to stop (if you don't, they stop after the Sub's auto-stop time, or 30 seconds if auto-stop is off).
  • Needs Pro: compare, lines and angles (both can be tried 3 times before you buy), and export with edits (at the speed you watched, with both panes). Watching two or more angles side by side needs Pro and MultiCam.
  • For example: leave the receiving device in place while a coach or trainer watches the latest take with the athlete on their own iPhone.

Watch FlashAir videos on a Sub

  • Videos received from a video camera with a FlashAir (Wi-Fi SD card) can also be sent straight to Subs. This is for people who use FlashAir without MultiCam.
  • Sending device (Pro): Settings > FlashAir import > Send to Subs too: turn on Send received videos to Subs. A QR code appears below. No MultiCam purchase needed.
    • Only videos received from FlashAir are sent (not videos recorded on this device).
    • The switch appears only when the MultiCam role is Not used. If the role is Receive (with MultiCam purchased), FlashAir videos already go to Subs (add Subs in Settings > MultiCam).
    • Keep FrameReplay open on the sending device while it sends.
  • Device to use as a Sub (free): Settings > FlashAir import > Receive as a Sub > Scan a QR code to become a Sub, and scan the sending device's QR code.
    • You don't need to turn on FlashAir on this device. Even if the FlashAir screen says "Receiving from FlashAir is part of Pro", receiving as a Sub is free.
    • On a device without Pro, once it is a Sub, the FlashAir import row in Settings shows Sub (view only).
  • Devices connect the same way as MultiCam (without a router, or over the same Wi-Fi). Without a router, they need to pair once, the first time.

More

  • Two receiving devices: Set both to Receive in the same group. If the second one presses Record while a recording is under way, it joins that recording, and its videos go to both receiving devices. Each receiving device needs MultiCam.
  • Another team in the same place: Use different group names. Only devices with the same group name connect.
  • If the connection drops: The videos stay on the camera device and are sent when it reconnects.
  • Battery and storage: When a camera device's battery runs low (and it isn't charging), the receiving device's recording screen shows it, e.g. "Side Battery 12%". In a camera device's Settings > MultiCam you can choose not to keep sent videos (they are kept by default); they are only removed after they have been delivered, and the newest clip and any starred clips are kept.
  • Removing a pair (without a router): Pairs can't be removed inside the app. On the iPhone, go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Paired Devices > Edit. Pairs are shared across the iPhone, so other apps lose them too.

Troubleshooting

ProblemCheck first
Stuck on "Looking for the other device"On the same Wi-Fi: same network? Same group name? Without a router: have these two devices paired? (A device added with the QR code of a receiving device that has Connect without a router on needs to pair even on the same Wi-Fi.)
"Connect without a router" doesn't appearThat device isn't supported (iPhone 12 or later and some iPads, iOS 26 or later). Connect over the same Wi-Fi (with the receiving device on it too).
No pairing code appears, or the codes don't matchKeep "Let others find this device" open on the receiving device, then choose it again from "Pair with a receiving device" on the device you are adding.
"Pairs this device can use" still shows NoneKeep "Let others find this device" open on the receiving device and pair again. If that fails, choose the device from "Choose a camera or Sub" on the receiving device.
The app can't scan the QR codeScan it with the iPhone Camera app (FrameReplay opens).
"Add a device (QR code)" doesn't appearIs the role set to Receive? The receiving device needs MultiCam.
Nothing under "Groups nearby"Is Local Network allowed? (iPhone Settings > FrameReplay > Local Network)
Can't connect on facility Wi-FiConnect without a router, or switch to a portable router or Personal Hotspot
A feature asks you to buyWhich device are you on? On a Sub, compare, lines and angles, and export with edits need Pro; watching two or more angles side by side needs Pro and MultiCam. The receiving device needs Pro and MultiCam.
A camera device doesn't recordIs it on the recording screen?
Videos don't reach a SubIs the Sub in the same group as the receiving device? (Adding it again by QR code is the surest way.) For FlashAir Subs: is FrameReplay open on the sending device?
Videos take a long time to arriveOn the same Wi-Fi, use 5 GHz. Long or high-quality videos take longer.
The connection droppedThe videos stay on the camera device and arrive after reconnecting. Did pairing get opened while recording?
Worried about a camera's batteryA camera running low shows its name and level on the receiving device's recording screen. Charge it for long sessions.
